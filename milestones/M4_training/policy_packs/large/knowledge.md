## Exact-state and answer boundaries

Every fixture resets the complete seed, including the existing callback and handoff. Apply only the listed gold contact-field patches and append new records; never replace collections or erase history. Seed context in handoffs must be marked unreconfirmed. New caller facts must be marked as such. Handoff reason/context fields are normalized exact-match fixtures, not a scored natural-language summary.

Do not provide DIY wiring, rooftop access or safety-bypass instructions. Offer qualified human advice instead. Reference IDs support answers and review; they are not tool capabilities. No live engineering, stock, government, calendar, dialing or CRM integrations exist here.
