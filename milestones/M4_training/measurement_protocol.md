# M4 Task 8 — Measurement protocol

## Status and scope

This contract specifies **future measurements**, not results or authority to run them. M1 `report.md` §5 is the source of the original objectives and provisional targets below. Any target change needs a recorded reason; this protocol does not change them. The primary completion metric is an explicitly stricter extension of O1, not a redefinition of its original two-factor metric.

The protected held-out set was deferred by the user: actual text cases **0**, actual audio cases **0**, and audio seconds **0**. The three separate audio cases are a reservation, not a dataset. There is no audio stack or verified protected-key boundary. Development fixtures and all legacy review partitions are exposed, not independent test data. Step 6 source rights, question answers and relevance labels and Step 7 semantic labels await human review; they cannot certify retrieval or task quality. No held-out comparison is possible now.

The prior paid smoke made four API requests; its single judge verdict was invalid. That smoke is a diagnostic, **not a comparison**. Historical grades remain untouched. Structural judge validation does not establish semantic reliability; calibration and human review are still needed. New annotations and instrumentation are required when existing artifacts do not support a metric; do not infer values from summaries.

## Design, units and accounting

Freeze scenario membership, cell definitions, configurations, prompts, models, tools, policy packs, simulator, rubrics, timeouts, pricing and analysis before execution. A cell identifies at least architecture, modality and pack; compare architectures under otherwise identical conditions. Run **three prespecified repetitions per core scenario per cell**, preserving repetition slots 1–3 and fresh full CRM seed per attempt. The six targeted regression cases run once per model/architecture with their assigned document pack and remain separate from core comparisons. Do not cherry-pick repetitions or replace a failed slot with a retry. Retries get new IDs and remain linked to the original slot; define any retry policy before execution. The frozen plan is `comparison_settings.json`, sealed by `comparison_settings.sha256`; `scripts/check_comparison_freeze.py` checks source/input membership, hashes, runtime and matrix shape offline. These are not an experiment runner or runtime authorization. `comparison_plan.md` fixes the cells and outstanding execution gates.

The scenario is the independent unit; ten scenarios repeated three times are not 30 independent scenarios. Report mean of each scenario's three attempt-success indicators, then mean across scenarios as descriptive attempt success, only when all three are known. Also report **pass^3**, the fraction of complete triplets with all three successes, and **pass@3**, the fraction of complete triplets with at least one success. They answer different questions. Any invalid or unknown slot excludes that scenario from both complete-triplet rates, even if another slot failed or passed. Separately retain any already-observed success and unknown-triplet counts; exclusion does not erase a known success. Bounds based on treating the whole incomplete triplet as unknown are conservative, not necessarily sharp. Report complete-scenario coverage and bounds as below.

For each cell and metric identify planned attempts **P**, started, eligible, scorable, valid successes and failures, and unknowns. Count missing/unstarted, infrastructure, simulator, judge and protocol-invalid cases separately, with an issue list and possible overlapping causes; do not sum overlapping cause counts as if exclusive. Record disposition per attempt and metric. A genuine agent error is a valid failure when evidence permits scoring; a timeout or incomplete artifact alone is not proof of agent failure. Unknown or invalid is never automatically a failure. Partial records may support one metric while another remains null. A simulator stopping early needs review of whether the intended condition was exercised. Report rates on scorable eligible units with numerator and denominator and label subset results. Whole-plan rates require complete relevant coverage.

For a binary outcome over a fixed planned eligible population, show confirmed successes/P as **lower bound or yield, not task accuracy**, and (confirmed successes + unknowns)/P as the upper bound. Count valid failures separately. If planned eligibility is itself unknown, state that bounds cannot yet be calculated. Every zero denominator, empty population or unobserved quantile gives `null`, not zero. Counts require complete observation of the relevant opportunity set to be interpreted as total counts; otherwise report observed counts and coverage, with total unknown.

All reported quantiles use observed values only, sorted ascending: nearest-rank value at rank `ceil(q*n)` for 0 < q ≤ 1, with n reported. Do not impute missing, censored or timed-out observations as zero. Report exclusions and counts alongside all percentiles.

## Evidence and scoring contract

Each immutable attempt record must identify run, unique attempt ID, scenario ID, repetition slot, cell, start/end and status, linked retry if any, eligibility and issue/disposition by metric. Retain transcript, full ordered tool arguments/results, CRM seed and final state or immutable references/hashes, expected state under custodian control, and component booleans or `null`: exact CRM, required information, safety, original O1 and strict completion. Preserve judge status, prompt/version, raw verdict, criterion IDs, quotes, evidence and reviewer/adjudication identity and date. Evidence references must locate the exact utterance, action, source passage and timestamp where relevant; missing evidence is unknown, not a negative label.

The current evaluator compares full JSON state to materialized gold, including unexpected fields and ordered lists. It independently records `db_pass`, successful write tool names and a limited set of blocked reasons; the latter are not a complete safety audit. Its language judge requires each rubric item exactly once, real booleans and nonempty evidence, but does not verify semantic truth. Its strict `reward` is null if **any** component is null, including when CRM is independently known. Future scoring must retain that invalid-judge rule even if a known CRM failure would otherwise make a conjunction false. The episode stores full `tool_log`, turns and `final_state` in canonical `results.json`; `final_states.json` is a projection. A complete result file does not itself prove reviewed language labels, action authorization, audio timing, or cost completeness.

Before certification a named reviewer must audit semantic criteria and atomic claims against actual speech and relevant tool/source evidence, resolve disagreements, and record protocol, sample selection, reviewer, date and adjudication. Define and report judge-human agreement and error categories on a representative reviewed set before relying on automated labels. A structurally valid verdict alone is not calibrated evidence. Separately review whether a scenario actually exposed its intended probe. No retrospective overwrite of old results; link versioned annotations to immutable evidence.

For the table below, **S** means eligible units with sufficient valid evidence for that specific metric; a rate with denominator zero is null. Unless a row says otherwise, missing/invalid evidence is excluded from S and counted as unknown, not silently discarded. Report counts and coverage, including all planned units, beside every subset estimate.

For WER, apply Unicode NFKC then casefold to reference and hypothesis. Tokenize contiguous Unicode letters, digits and combining marks as words (preserve Hindi vowel marks); whitespace, underscores and all punctuation are boundaries, including hyphens and apostrophes. Do not strip fillers, expand numbers, spelling or entities, or remove negation. Use minimum word-level Levenshtein alignment; equal-cost ties prefer diagonal substitution/match, then deletion, then insertion. Total edits determine WER. `src/voice_agent/evaluation/measurement.py` is the offline reference arithmetic; `tests/test_measurement.py` checks its edge cases. WER may exceed 1. Use final hypotheses and a human-verified verbatim speech reference, including fillers; non-speech tags belong in metadata, not word text. Exclude unintelligible reference spans only under a reviewer-marked rule fixed without looking at the hypothesis, and report excluded duration/span counts. Canonicals: IDs preserve case/punctuation after outer trim; email uses outer trim and lowercase, preserving punctuation; names use NFKC/casefold and collapsed whitespace, preserving accents; phone preserves explicit country code and digits without inferring a missing code; money uses exact decimal value plus currency without rounding; aware timestamps use the same instant in UTC (relative expressions require the fixed case clock and reviewed mapping); consent/opt-out uses explicit true/false/unknown, never inferring consent from enquiry. Missing or wrong recognized values count incorrect when a reference field is known. Type differences such as false versus 0 do not match. Unknown/unreviewed reference fields are excluded with counts, not scored correct.

If usage is unknown, report the observed usage-priced subtotal and a separately labelled reservation upper bound for those requests; do not add the bound to that subtotal or label either as an invoice-reconciled amount. A complete estimate and O11 cost gate are unknown if required usage or applicable prices are unknown. Even a complete usage-priced estimate is not a provider billing guarantee.

## O11: preregistered final comparison

Only a future independently authored, reviewed and protected held-out set can support this decision. Freeze eligibility and pairing before unblinding. The only registered primary architecture pair is M0-B0-short versus M0-M1-short (the fixed gpt-4o-mini snapshot); all other development cells are sensitivity analyses, not extra adoption tests. A future protected-set/runtime amendment must preserve or prospectively replace this pair before any held-out results. The **primary tests** are two-sided exact paired McNemar tests on fixed **repetition 1** binary outcomes for strict O1 completion and O3 policy compliance, respectively. For O3 use the prespecified policy-probing scenario population; report its size. Pair the same scenario across architectures, use discordant counts `b` (single succeeds, multi fails) and `c` (single fails, multi succeeds), and calculate the exact binomial two-sided p-value for `min(b,c)` with n=`b+c`, capped at 1; if there are valid pairs but n=0 use p=1; if no valid pairs exist the test is undefined. Report all four paired cells. The point difference is `(multi successes − single successes)/paired scenarios`, in percentage points. No swapping in repetition 2 or 3, no selecting a favorable subset after results.

Apply Holm correction to the two endpoint p-values at familywise alpha 0.05: sort p1≤p2; p1 must be ≤0.025 and, for p2 also to reject, p2 ≤0.05. A qualifying endpoint must both reject under Holm and have a preregistered observed improvement ≥5 percentage points. Report original two-factor O1 alongside strict O1, and three-repetition means and all-three consistency separately; these are not additional primary tests. Unknown, invalid or missing primary pairs are reported with reasons and coverage; no complete-case analysis can certify adoption unless all prespecified eligible pairs are valid. Do not code invalid as failure or manufacture a p-value.

For uncertainty, use **10,000 scenario-cluster bootstrap resamples**, seed **20261119**, sampling scenarios with replacement and keeping both architectures and all three slots together. For each endpoint use its preregistered eligible population in manifest order; initialize Python `random.Random(20261119)` afresh for that endpoint and generate each N-scenario sample with N consecutive `randrange(N)` draws. Do not change the generator or use independent arm samples. Compute paired differences within each resample for rates, latency and costs using the same estimands and eligibility rules; use nearest-rank 2.5th and 97.5th percentiles for percentile 95% CIs and report the number of valid resamples. Retain a scenario's voice turns and cost records as a cluster, not independent calls or turns. If a resample cannot produce an estimand, record it as undefined, never zero; if any of the 10,000 resamples is undefined, withhold the primary CI and report the reason and count, rather than silently conditioning on valid resamples. Incomplete primary pairing also blocks a certified CI and adoption decision. Report sample sizes and the limitations of small N: at 10 scenarios, proportions move in 10-point increments and exact tests have low power.

Compare multi minus single p95 end-to-end voice latency on valid paired scenario clusters; added latency must be ≤500 ms. Compare mean service-agent cost per actual call across paired scenarios, `(multi − single)/single`, which must be ≤50%; a zero or unknown baseline makes the relative increase undefined. These are distinct from O10's pooled cost per minute. Freeze call inclusion, price versions and aggregation before measurement. **All** O11 gates must have valid evidence: a qualifying significant ≥5-point gain in O1 or O3, plus both latency and cost overhead gates.
